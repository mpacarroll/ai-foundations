# Five First Exercises

Short, real, and doable today. Each takes a few minutes. Do them with any AI assistant open in front of you. These five are a taste; the full thirty are in *The AI Practice Book*.

## 1. The rewrite
Take the longest message or email you sent this week. Paste it in and say: *"Cut this to half the length without losing anything that matters."* Read both. Notice what it kept. **You are learning:** AI is a fast editor, and shorter is usually better.

## 2. The explainer
Ask it to explain something you already understand deeply — your job, a hobby, how to cook something you make often. Grade the answer in your head. **You are learning:** where it is sharp and where it is vague, so you know when to trust it.

## 3. The interviewer
Pick a decision you are chewing on. Say: *"Interview me about this decision. Ask one question at a time. Do not give advice yet."* Answer honestly for a few rounds. **You are learning:** AI is often more useful asking than answering.

## 4. The three-options trick
Next time you want it to write something, do not ask for "a" version. Ask for *three* — "give me three versions in different tones and let me pick." **You are learning:** options beat a single guess, every time.

## 5. The honesty check
Ask it for five facts about a topic you know well. Look each one up. Mark them true, false, or misleading. **You are learning:** the single most important habit — verify facts, trust it for drafts. You stay in charge.
